📘 How to Redeem a Promo Code

Choose the steps for your device. The game's menus and promotion rules can change, so follow the current in-game instructions.
🖥️

PC & Android

Redeem from the game when the Promo Codes option is available.
  1. Open Mech Arena and sign in to the account that will receive the reward.
  2. Open your Inbox, then choose Promo Codes.
  3. Paste an eligible code and confirm it.
  4. Check the result and claim the reward from your in-game Inbox.
📱

iPhone & iPad

Some iOS redemptions continue in the official activation page.
  1. In Mech Arena, open your Inbox and tap Promo Codes.
  2. If the game opens an activation page, enter your Player ID and the code there.
  3. Confirm the redemption, return to the game and check your Inbox for the reward.
New Player codes may have a short redemption window, often the first 24 hours after account creation. Check each code’s eligibility and any campaign-specific rules. Only enter account details on an official page opened by the game; never share your password or OTP.

⌕Where can I find new codes?+

There is no fixed release schedule. Codes may come with updates, events or official campaigns. Check MechNova’s current code list and read each code’s eligibility and status.

!Why isn't my code working?+

It may have expired, be for another account type, already be redeemed, or have a cooldown. Copy it exactly and check the code’s notes. The game’s message is the final guide.

🎁Where are my rewards?+

Make sure the game accepted the code, then open your in-game Inbox and claim the reward message. If it still does not arrive, contact official in-game support. Never share your password or OTP.

◷Can I redeem multiple codes?+

Different eligible codes may be redeemed over time, but cooldowns or account limits can apply—sometimes around 24 hours. New Player codes are often limited to eligible new accounts. Follow the message shown in-game.

↻How often are new codes released?+

There is no guaranteed schedule. Watch for official updates, seasonal events and creator campaigns, and check the current list before redeeming because codes can expire.
