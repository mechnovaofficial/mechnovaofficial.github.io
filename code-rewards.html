REWARD BREAKDOWN

What Can Mech Arena Promo Codes Reward?

A reward list is only useful when you can tell what is being offered, who can claim it, and whether the publisher has actually confirmed the terms. Use this guide to evaluate reward claims before spending time on a redemption attempt.

Guide reviewed: 20 October 2026. Rewards and campaign rules can change; this page does not independently test every code.

💎

A-Coins

Premium in-game currency. Confirm the amount, the campaign conditions and whether the offer is still available before planning an upgrade around it.

🪙

Credits

In-game currency. Consider the reward in the context of your current hangar and the upgrades you actually intend to make.

🎁

Crates & Keys

Crates may contain variable items. A crate name or picture is not proof of a guaranteed specific item inside.

🤖

Mechs, Weapons & Skins

Some promotions mention a mech, weapon or skin but may be an event reward, store pack or account-limited offer rather than a universal redeem code.

First identify what kind of offer it is

Offer typeWhat it usually meansWhat to verify
Promo codeA code is entered through the redemption method stated by the game or campaign.Exact code, expiry, account eligibility, region/platform restrictions and redemption result.
Store or starter packA link opens an offer page; it may have purchase or new-account conditions.Price, purchase terms, account requirements and whether the offer is actually free.
Event rewardA reward is earned by playing, reaching a milestone, opening a crate or completing an event condition.Event dates, required actions, resource cost, milestone requirements and disclosed odds.

These offer types are not interchangeable. A video thumbnail that shows a mech does not prove that a universal promo code grants that mech. Check the offer itself, not just the headline, image or repost.

How to verify a reward claim

  1. Find the closest primary source. Start with the current in-game News or offer screen and official Mech Arena or Plarium announcements. Community posts and videos can help you discover a claim, but they are not the final authority.
  2. Record the date. Note when the source was published or when you checked it. An old code list can remain online after a campaign has ended.
  3. Read the eligibility conditions. Check whether the offer is for all players, new accounts, returning accounts or selected accounts. Look for region, platform, account-age and expiry restrictions.
  4. Compare the exact reward terms. Check quantities and item names. Keep guaranteed rewards separate from possible crate contents, bonus items or promotional artwork.
  5. Confirm the outcome in-game. A successful submission message and the resulting inventory or Inbox entry are stronger evidence than a copied list. If the offer fails, record the error instead of labelling it universally active.

How to judge different reward types

A-Coins and Credits

Do not evaluate currency only by the number shown in a post. Check whether the amount is part of a redeemable code, a milestone, a purchase offer or a possible crate result. Then consider whether you can use that resource toward a real upgrade goal. Never spend currency on the assumption that an unverified code will reimburse you later.

Crates and random rewards

Read the complete possible-reward panel and look for published odds or guaranteed milestones. If the odds are not disclosed, there is no sound basis for calculating a guaranteed result or an average number of openings. Set a hard spending limit before opening anything.

Mechs, weapons and cosmetics

Confirm whether the named item is directly granted, supplied as a blueprint, offered through a pack, or merely shown in event artwork. A rank or upgrade level matters, too: the item mentioned in a promotion may not be the same as a fully upgraded item shown in a video.

⚠️

Don't treat a list as a guarantee

MechNova is an independent fan resource. A listed reward or status label is not a live test of every account, and MechNova cannot change publisher eligibility or restore an expired promotion.

Common questions

Does a picture of a mech mean the code gives that mech?

No. Verify the exact written reward and offer type. Images may show a featured item rather than a guaranteed redemption reward.

Why can a code work for one player but not another?

Campaign conditions can differ by account, region, platform, prior redemption or expiry. Use the publisher's terms and the in-game message to identify the likely reason.
