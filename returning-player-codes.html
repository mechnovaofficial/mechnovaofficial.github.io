Mech Arena Returning Player Codes

Returning-player promotions may target selected accounts or players who meet campaign-specific conditions. The phrase “returning player” does not tell you the exact inactivity period or who qualifies; only current campaign terms can confirm that.

How to check a returning-player offer

  1. Find the publisher's original announcement or campaign terms.
  2. Check the required inactivity period, account eligibility and expiry date, if stated.
  3. Confirm whether the offer is a promo code or a separate event/store offer.
  4. Redeem through the official game or publisher flow only.

Codes listed for returning players

MechNova's homepage listing includes IMBACKBABY and BLASTZONE in its returning-player category. This is a dated listing, not a guarantee that either code will work on every returning account. Verify the terms and check the in-game result.
