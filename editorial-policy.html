Editorial Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

MechNova publishes independent gaming information about Mech Arena promo codes, rewards, eligibility, packs and gameplay. This page explains how we aim to present that information clearly. Start with our Mech Arena promo-code guide or browse the dated code list on the homepage.

Information sources and status

Promo-code information may be gathered from publisher announcements, creator campaigns, community updates and other publicly available sources. A code shown in a list is not necessarily confirmed to redeem successfully at the time you visit. Where applicable, we identify the source-list date and distinguish listed status from independently verified live redemption.

Rewards, eligibility, account-age limits, region restrictions and expiry can vary by campaign. The game publisher's current terms and in-game confirmation take precedence over third-party summaries.

We distinguish three dates where relevant: when a source published a claim, when MechNova last reviewed a page or source link, and when an in-game redemption was actually checked. A page review date does not mean every code was tested in-game on that date. We should only describe an in-game check as completed when a check date has been recorded; otherwise, readers should treat the entry as a dated reference and confirm it in their own game.

Community forum discussions and creator videos may help us discover a code or offer, but they are not automatically proof of current availability. We aim to link to primary publisher guidance when available, distinguish guaranteed rewards from possible crate contents, and avoid presenting an unannounced campaign as an active code.

Updates and corrections

We may revise code listings, reward descriptions and guides when new information becomes available. If you find an error, outdated status or unclear eligibility note, send the exact page URL, the claim that needs checking and a supporting public source through our Contact page or official community channels on Instagram or Discord. Reports can be reviewed before a correction is made; a submitted report does not guarantee a change.

Editorial independence and monetization

MechNova is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Plarium or the Mech Arena developers. If affiliate links or paid promotions are included, they should be identified clearly. External Plarium offer URLs may carry campaign or creator attribution parameters; an external campaign link does not, by itself, establish a MechNova sponsorship or referral relationship. Monetization does not change the publisher's official eligibility or reward terms.

Useful and responsible information

Our aim is to provide practical context rather than promise free rewards or guaranteed outcomes. Never share game passwords, one-time codes or payment details with unofficial redemption services. Use official game and publisher instructions whenever available.
