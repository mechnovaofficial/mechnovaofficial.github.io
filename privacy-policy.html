Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This notice describes the website’s current features and the information that may be processed when you visit MechNova. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions.

What the site does

You can browse public pages without creating a MechNova visitor account. Promo-code search and display run in the page. The site connects to Firebase services to load public promo-code/site-setting data; a restricted administrator area uses Firebase Authentication. Do not enter game passwords, one-time codes, payment details or account recovery information on this site.

Promo-code “Report issue” links open a prefilled email draft in your device’s email app. They do not send anything automatically. If you choose to send the message, your email provider processes it and MechNova receives the details you include. Review or edit the draft first, and do not include passwords, OTPs, payment details or account recovery information.

Information handled by providers

GitHub Pages serves the website. Firebase, used for the public site data and restricted administrator area, may process technical request information and administrator sign-in data under Google’s terms and privacy practices. The site also loads Google Fonts to display its typography, the Google Translate language widget, YouTube thumbnail images and Amazon product images. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser/device details and the requested resource when your browser loads their content. If you follow a social, video or retailer link, that provider may process information under its own privacy policy.

Provider practices, cookies and retention are controlled by those providers. You can use browser settings to manage cookies and block third-party content, though some features or images may then not load.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

MechNova offers optional Google Analytics 4 measurement to understand page visits and interactions such as promo-code copy clicks, video clicks and gaming-gear link clicks. The analytics script is not loaded unless you choose “Accept analytics” in the consent notice. If you decline, MechNova does not load that analytics tag. If you accept, Google Analytics may process usage and device/browser information and use cookies or similar technologies under Google’s terms and privacy practices. The website disables Google Signals and ad-personalization signals in this analytics tag; this does not affect separate services such as AdSense, YouTube or retailer links, which are controlled by their own settings and policies.

You can change your selection at any time using the control below. Amazon affiliate links may separately record qualifying referrals or purchases through Amazon’s systems.

Sharing, retention and security

MechNova does not sell visitor information. Site information is handled by the providers needed to run the website and its features, or may be disclosed when legally required. Public promo codes and site settings are stored as site data; administrator authentication is handled by Firebase. Provider retention periods and security controls are described in their own documentation. No online service can promise absolute security.

Your requests and children

If you have a privacy question or request relating to information you provided to MechNova, email krishnanare9@gmail.com or use the Contact page, and identify the relevant page or account context. We will review the request and respond by email. This is a general gaming-information site and does not offer public visitor accounts or knowingly ask children to submit personal information. Parents or guardians can contact us if they believe a child has sent information.

Changes

We may revise this policy when the site or its providers change. The latest revision date appears above. Please review this page periodically for the latest notice.
