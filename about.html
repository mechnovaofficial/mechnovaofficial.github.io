About MechNova

I’m Krushna Zanak Narhe. I play Mech Arena regularly, run the MechNova YouTube channel and maintain this independent player guide.

Why I started collecting codes

I wanted to claim a reward and get back to playing. Instead, I kept searching different sources for the same code. Finding the letters was only half the job. I still had to work out whether the offer was for my account, whether it had ended and what would actually arrive in my hangar. I built MechNova to put those answers beside the copy button.

What I look for when I test a code

I read the offer, check which account it describes and compare the claim with the reward list. One example from my channel is TRICKORFEAR. My October 30 upload listed 200 A-Coins, 31,100 Credits and 13 Fortune Keys. The keys matter here. Spending them later is another action, and the result of that opening should never be added to the original code’s reward list. Watch the dated TRICKORFEAR video.

I can show what happened on my account. I can’t use that result to promise the same reward on a newly registered account, a returning player’s hangar or the same account weeks later. Codes expire. Check the source, eligibility and date on a listing before using an attempt. Where an expiry is unknown, an old video cannot supply the missing deadline.

Beyond the copy button

I also upload gameplay and event videos. In my October Nomad Event Crate Rush coverage, the opening screen showed a price of 350 A-Coins. That number let me work out how many openings a balance could cover. It told me nothing about the odds of getting Nomad. The event guide and budget calculator let you repeat that calculation with the price on your own screen.

Who runs the site and how to reach me

MechNova is my independent fan project. Plarium operates the game, sets eligibility and handles missing in-game items. I handle the writing, directory and corrections here. If a listing is wrong, send the page, offer name, date and non-sensitive result through Contact. The editorial policy explains how sources, corrections and commercial links are handled.
