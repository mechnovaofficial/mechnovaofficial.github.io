How the project works

MechNova is built for players who want to spend less time searching through scattered code posts and more time understanding the terms of a promotion. The site brings together code references, redemption help, reward explainers, event-cost guidance and gameplay resources.

Time-sensitive information is treated as time-sensitive: an old listing, screenshot or video does not prove that an offer is available today. Where a claim cannot be confirmed from a current publisher source or an in-game result, it should be presented as a dated reference or an unverified report—not as a guarantee.

What MechNova can and cannot confirm

MechNova can explain how to check an offer, organise reported information and publish independent gameplay guidance. It cannot issue promo codes, change account eligibility, guarantee a reward, or reverse an expired campaign. Final availability and redemption results are controlled by the game publisher.

We also aim to distinguish redeemable codes from store packs, event milestones and crate rewards. These may have different conditions and should not be described as the same type of offer.

Corrections and community feedback

If you spot a broken link, unclear instruction, outdated status or incorrect reward description, send the exact page URL, code or event name, and the non-sensitive error text through the Contact page. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, recovery details or payment information.

Read our Editorial Policy to understand how MechNova approaches sources, updates, corrections and commercial links.
