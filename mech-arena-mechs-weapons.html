Mech Arena Mechs and Weapons Guide

Choosing a mech and weapon combination depends on playstyle, hangar progress, upgrade level, mode and current game balance. No single loadout is universally best.

How to compare mechs

  • Role: identify whether the mech suits aggressive, defensive, support or mobile play.
  • Ability: understand its cooldown, range and tactical trade-offs.
  • Energy capacity and rank: compare your upgrades and compatible weapons in-game.
  • Mode and map: a build that works in one mode may perform differently elsewhere.

How to compare weapons

Check range, reload, magazine, damage pattern, energy cost and synergy with the mech's role. Use current in-game descriptions because balance changes can make older videos outdated.
