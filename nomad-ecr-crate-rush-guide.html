How to decide whether a spin is worth it

Choose the reward you actually want

Is Nomad the main target, or would blueprints and currency also be useful for your current Hangar? Decide before you open the crate, not after seeing an outcome.

Check the full possible-reward panel

Read the live in-game reward list, eligibility notes and any event rules. A reward preview is not the same as a guaranteed reward, and one screenshot may not show all terms.

Look for odds and guaranteed milestones

If exact drop rates or a guaranteed milestone are not clearly disclosed, do not invent a probability or assume a fixed number of spins will unlock Nomad. Ask in-game or consult an official event announcement.

Set a hard A-Coins limit

Pick the maximum amount you are willing to spend before opening anything. Do not chase a previous loss by adding more spins, and do not spend coins needed for a more important upgrade.

Compare with other ways to use your currency

Before spending, compare the crate with other currently available in-game offers or upgrades you already planned. Do not assume a crate is better value merely because it is a limited-time event.

Frequently asked questions

Does one 350 A-Coins spin guarantee Nomad?

No such guarantee is visible in the screenshot. Treat Nomad as a possible reward unless the current in-game event terms explicitly promise otherwise.

Can I calculate the chance of getting Nomad?

Only if the game publishes reliable drop rates or enough information to calculate the chance. The supplied screenshot does not show those odds, so a precise probability would be speculation.

How do I know when the event ends?

Use the live countdown and details shown in your current in-game event screen. A screenshot can become outdated after it is captured.

Should I spend all my A-Coins?

That depends on your goals and available currency, but spending everything on a random reward is risky. Set a limit first and keep enough currency for upgrades you already value.
