Mech Arena New Player Codes

Some Mech Arena promotions are restricted to newly created accounts or a specific starter campaign. A code listed online is not guaranteed to work for every new account: account-age windows and other eligibility rules may apply.

Before trying a new-player code

  • Check the original source and publication date.
  • Read any account-creation deadline or new-player condition.
  • Confirm the stated reward instead of relying on a thumbnail or old video.
  • Use only the game's official redemption flow or an official Plarium page.

Codes listed for new players

MechNova's homepage listing includes GRASSHOPPER, CHILLBOT, WARTEX, BACKTRACK, ROBOTRUSH and BIGMECH in its new-player category. Treat these as listed codes, not a guarantee of current redemption. Campaign terms can change and the game decides account eligibility.

Why might a code fail?

The account may not qualify, the campaign may have ended, the code may already have been redeemed, or the offer may have platform or regional limits. Check official instructions before retrying.
